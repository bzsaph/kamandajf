Cargo & Truck Logistics

From Europe to Rwanda, One Partner

Importing machinery, vehicles, stock or personal effects? We handle the whole journey: collection in Europe, ocean freight to East Africa and trucking inland to your warehouse, site or home in Rwanda.

  1. Collection in Europe

    Pickup from suppliers or the ports of Antwerp, Rotterdam and Hamburg.

  2. Ocean Freight to East Africa

    FCL or groupage shipping to Mombasa (Kenya) or Dar es Salaam (Tanzania).

  3. Road Transit by Truck

    Northern Corridor via Nairobi and Kampala, or Central Corridor via Rusumo, with the cargo tracked all the way.

  4. Clearance & Delivery in Rwanda

    Customs coordination in Kigali and final delivery to your door.
