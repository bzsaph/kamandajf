Trucking & Logistics

Full Logistics: Local, Africa & Worldwide

Trucking and logistics are at the heart of what we do. We move goods of every kind, including containers, building materials, stock, machinery and household goods, within Rwanda, across Africa and from Europe, Asia and the USA, with experienced drivers and every load tracked.

We ship from
Rwanda (local) Africa Europe Asia (China, India, UAE) USA
Trucking routes
Kigali – Mombasa Kigali – Dar es Salaam Kigali – Kampala Kigali – Goma / Bukavu Kigali – Bujumbura All provinces of Rwanda
Importing to Rwanda, step by step
  1. Collection Worldwide

    Pickup from suppliers and ports in Europe, Asia, the USA and across Africa.

  2. Sea or Air Freight

    Sea freight (full container or groupage) to Mombasa or Dar es Salaam, or air freight straight to Kigali International Airport.

  3. Road Transit by Truck

    Northern Corridor via Nairobi and Kampala, or Central Corridor via Rusumo, with the cargo tracked all the way.

  4. Clearance & Delivery in Rwanda

    Customs coordination in Kigali and final delivery to your door.
